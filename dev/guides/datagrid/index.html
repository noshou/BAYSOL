DataGrid

BAYSOL.DataGrid is a module of its own (src/data-grid/). SHANNON_REBIN and BIN_BIAS_MAX are fixed constants of the module (not settings); it has no params.jl. The physical constants it shares with the other modules are in PhysicalConstants (physical-constants.jl at the root of src/); caching, timing, GC pausing and threading live in Runtime; point sets, excluded volumes and SASA in Geometry.

The data reduction that precedes the fit. For a particle whose scatterers lie in a set of diameter D,

\[I(q)=\int_0^D p(r)\,\frac{\sin qr}{qr}\,dr\]

with p(r) the distance distribution, zero beyond D because the autocorrelation of a function supported on a set of diameter D vanishes beyond D. Hence F(q) = qI(q) = ∫₀^D (p(r)/r) sin(qr) dr is the Fourier transform of a function supported on [−D, D] (odd extension), and the Whittaker–Shannon theorem (expand it in a Fourier series on [−D, D]) gives

\[F(q)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}F\!\Big(\frac{n\pi}{D}\Big)\,\mathrm{sinc}\!\Big(\frac{Dq}{\pi}-n\Big)\]

The curve is fully determined by its values on the grid Δq = π/D (one Shannon channel), so a range [qMin, qMax] holds only N_s = (qMax − qMin)·D/π independent values. Fitting every point makes the Gaussian likelihood claim far more independent data than exist, and it is an upper bound on how many parameters the curve can constrain. N_s is nChannels of ShannonInfo, printed in the report.

  • cloudDiameter(points): the exact diameter of a (3, n) cloud. A convex function's maximum over the hull is at a vertex, so the farthest pair lies on the convex hull; it is built with the module's own 3-D quickhull (quick-hull.jl: initial tetrahedron from the coordinate extremes, then the visible faces and horizon of each farthest point) and only its vertices (~100 of thousands) are compared pairwise (fewer than four points or a degenerate cloud is compared directly). seedModel applies it to the atoms and the hydration beads (the actual scatterer cloud, 5–6 Å wider than the atoms alone).

  • shannonData(q, I, σ; D, rebin, maxBinBias, lMax, dropNonpositive): inverse-variance binning to rebin bins per channel (width π/(rebin·D)), each bin carrying

    \[\bar q=\frac{\sum w_jq_j}{\sum w_j},\quad \bar I=\frac{\sum w_jI_j}{\sum w_j},\quad \bar\sigma=\Big(\sum w_j\Big)^{-1/2},\quad w_j=\sigma_j^{-2}\]

    This is the minimum-variance unbiased linear estimator (Gauss–Markov; Lagrange multiplier on Σλ_j = 1 gives λ_j ∝ w_j) and keeps Σw·I and Σw, the sufficient statistics of the linear fit, unchanged: by the weighted analysis-of-variance identity binning is lossless exactly when the model is constant over the bin. It assumes independent errors; correlated errors make σ̄ too small (the variance of an equicorrelated mean tends to ρ_cσ², not 0), which is why the binned χ² of a smooth misfit exceeds the raw one by about the number of points per bin and the report quotes χ² on the measured grid. Bins with non-positive mean are dropped (dropNonpositive = true); this removes the negative half of the noise at high q, biasing the survivors upward by ≈ 0.8σ̄ at a true value 0, 0.06σ̄ at 2σ̄ and < 0.005σ̄ above 3σ̄.

  • Bin bias and rebin. A bin is the mean of the curve over its width, not its value at the mean q: E[Ī] − I(q̄) ≈ ½ I″ Var_w(q) = (Δ²/24) I″ (relative: Δ²/24 · |I″/I|). Taking |I″/I| ≤ (D/2)² gives the worst-case relative bias π²/(96·rebin²) (7.1·10⁻⁴ at 12); this is a design heuristic (it holds for the monotone decay of compact particles, not as a theorem). binBiasRatio divides it by the smallest binned relative error; shannonData raises rebin (k ← max(k+1, ⌈k√(ℛ_k/0.3)⌉), at most 64 times, using β_k ∝ k⁻²) until the ratio is at most BIN_BIAS_MAX = 0.3 (maxBinBias = Inf keeps rebin as given), and fits the curve unbinned once the binned one holds at least 90 % as many points. SHANNON_REBIN = 12 is the default and a minimum; the report prints the rebin used and the achieved bin bias (warn above 0.3).

  • Band limit. autoLmax(D, qMax) = max(1, ⌈qMax·D⌉): the degree-l moment of a scatterer at r ≤ D is ∝ j_l(qr), negligible once l exceeds qMax D by a few x^{1/3} (Scattering); conservative by up to 2 for a centred cloud, safe for any origin. The moment count grows as (lMax+1)², a further reason to bin before building the forward model. For D = 100 Å, q ∈ [0.01, 0.3]: N_s ≈ 9.2, ~110 binned points, lMax = 30.

  • modelOnRaw(info, y): the model on the measured grid by an interpolating cubic spline (not-a-knot, solved in the module as one tridiagonal system; error 0.001σ for 1 % data at the default rebin), giving the reduced χ² on the measured points that a depositor's χ² refers to.

  • residualStructure(r): lag-1 autocorrelation (StatsBase.jl) and Wald–Wolfowitz runs-test z (hand-written, a few lines) of the normalised residuals. White residuals have both ≈ 0; a smooth misfit of the conventional model gives large values. Not in the report; bysl fits diagnose --residuals ID prints them.
