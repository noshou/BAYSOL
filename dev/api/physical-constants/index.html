Physical constants

The module BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants (physical-constants.jl at the root of src/, next to BAYSOL.jl and precompile.jl; bound at the package root, using BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants: AVOGADRO). Every physical constant and unit conversion, defined once so it cannot drift between modules; other modules import by name (using ..PhysicalConstants: AVOGADRO, WATER_MOLAR_MASS).

Physical constants and units shared across BAYSOL.

Å per metre.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.AVOGADRO — Constant

Avogadro constant, mol⁻¹ (CODATA, exact since the 2019 SI redefinition).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.BACKBONE_ELECTRONS — Constant

Peptide backbone unit (-CH2CONH-, neutral, C2H3NO) electron count: 2×C(6) + 3×H(1) + N(7) + O(8) = 30 e. Added once per residue on the same basis as BACKBONE_PMV; Protein.json's electron_count field is a side-chain-only increment relative to glycine.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.CM3_PER_LITER — Constant

cm³ per litre.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.ELEMENTARY_CHARGE — Constant

Elementary charge e, C (exact since the 2019 SI redefinition).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.EV_EXP_COEFF — Constant

Coefficient of the excluded-volume exponent, (4π/3)^(2/3) / 4π, used by excludedVolumeFactor and by the c1 profiling.

Converts CRYSOL's radius parameterisation into the volume parameterisation _gaussianDummy is written in, via V = (4π/3) r³ (which is exactly Geometry.sphereVolume, so rM and the dummy volumes stay consistent).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.FOUR_PI — Constant

The solid angle of the sphere, 4π: the prefactor of the partial-wave expansion and the s = q/4π of the form factors.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.HC_EV_ANGSTROM — Constant

h·c in eV·Å, from the exact SI values of h, c and e: ≈ 12398.41984. Photon energy from wavelength: E (eV) = HC_EV_ANGSTROM / λ (Å).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.KELL_DENSITY_DEN — Constant

Denominator coefficient b of the Kell equation (see KELL_DENSITY_NUM), °C⁻¹.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.KELL_DENSITY_NUM — Constant

Numerator coefficients (a₀, …, a₅) of the Kell equation (1975) for the mass density of pure water at 1 atm, kg·m⁻³, valid 0–150 °C:

ρ(t) = (a₀ + a₁t + a₂t² + a₃t³ + a₄t⁴ + a₅t⁵) / (1 + b·t), t in °C

with b = KELL_DENSITY_DEN.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.KELVIN_OFFSET — Constant

Kelvin minus °C: T (K) = t (°C) + KELVIN_OFFSET (absolute zero is −KELVIN_OFFSET °C).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.LN10 — Constant

Natural logarithm of 10.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.LOG_TWO_PI — Constant

Natural logarithm of 2π, log(2π): the constant of the Gaussian log density.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.MS_PER_S — Constant

Milliseconds per second.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.NM_INV_PER_ANGSTROM_INV — Constant

nm⁻¹ per Å⁻¹ (1 Å⁻¹ = 10 nm⁻¹). A curve deposited in nm⁻¹ is converted with q ./ NM_INV_PER_ANGSTROM_INV. (An integer, so the division is bit-identical to q ./ 10.)

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.NS_PER_S — Constant

Nanoseconds per second: converts time_ns() and Base's *_time_ns() counters to seconds.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.PLANCK_CONSTANT — Constant

Planck constant h, J·s (exact since the 2019 SI redefinition).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.PM_PER_ANGSTROM — Constant

pm per Å (the bundled ionic-radius table is in pm).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.SPEED_OF_LIGHT — Constant

Speed of light in vacuum c, m·s⁻¹ (exact).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.STANDARD_TEMPERATURE_C — Constant

Standard reference temperature, °C: 298.15 K, the temperature standard-state data (e.g. the bundled partial-molar-volume tables) are at.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.UNIT_OF_δρ — Constant

Shell-contrast unit in e·Å⁻³ (CRYSOL's –dro); ρ_k = UNIT_OF_δρ * δρ_k.

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.WATER_DENSITY_UNCERTAINTY — Constant

Absolute uncertainty on the Kell water density, kg·m⁻³ (≈ 20 ppm of ρ, nearly constant 0-150 °C).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.WATER_ELECTRONS — Constant

Electrons per H₂O molecule (2 from H, 8 from O).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.WATER_MOLAR_MASS — Constant

Molar mass of H₂O, g·mol⁻¹ (IAPWS-95 value).

source
BAYSOL.PhysicalConstants.energyEv — Method
energyEv(value, unit)

The photon energy in eV of value read in unit: "eV" as it is, "keV" times 1000, "nm" and "A" a wavelength (nm, Å), HC_EV_ANGSTROM over the wavelength in Å.

Returns

  • The energy in eV, a Float64.

Exceptions

  • ArgumentError for a unit that is none of the four.
