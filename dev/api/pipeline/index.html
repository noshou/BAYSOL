Pipeline: entry points

The pipeline entry points seedModel and runModel and their result types (src/pipeline/): seedModel loads the structure and the data and builds the forward cache and priors, and runModel samples the posterior. (runProject, which does all of it for every model of a project folder and writes the run folder, belongs to the terminal interface: tui/src/store/run-project.jl.) The package root re-binds the entry points (including results, writeLog and printSummary) as BAYSOL.seedModel etc. The figures and the workbook are on Pipeline: results and figures, the run log, summary and tables on Pipeline: run log and tables, and the tunable constants and the settings as keywords on Pipeline: settings.

BAYSOL.Pipeline.MAPParams — Type
MAPParams = Dict{String, Float64}

Parameter values at the single MAP (maximum a posteriori, i.e. highest-log-density non-divergent) draw found by runModel, keyed by name:

  • logDensity: the MAP draw's log-posterior density.
  • slvntEDns, deltaRho1, deltaRho2, deltaRho3: the shell parameters at that draw (see Inference.Seed for the ξ ordering this is read off of).
  • cavityShellFrac: fraction of the hydration-shell volume carried by cavity beads (from the Gram matrix at the lowest q). When it is ~0, δρ₃ has essentially no likelihood and its posterior is just its prior.
  • scale, bkgrndcorr: the WLS-fit detector scale/background at that draw.
  • chisqred: the WLS fit's reduced χ² at that draw.
  • zSlvntEDns, zDeltaRho1, zDeltaRho2, zDeltaRho3: how many prior standard deviations (θ-space) the corresponding physical parameter's MAP value sits from its prior mean.

BAYSOL.Pipeline.seedModel — Method

Computes a NUTS seed from given data input:

0.  keep the part of the measured curve to fit (`qMin`, `qMax`, `excludeQ`) and
    scale its errors (`sigmaScale`)
1.  resolve molSrc to a structure (the `model` of it, and only its `chains`)
2.  look the residue names up (`components`) and decide the hydrogenation from
    them (`classify`); if addHydrogens, add hydrogens with Pdb2pqr: protein only,
    PARSE with PROPKA's pKas (they drive the terminus protonation); nucleotides
    present, AMBER and no PROPKA run if there is no protein; modified
    nucleotides are hydrogenated through the standard residue of their base
3.  compute the accessible surface (the hydration-shell beads), take
    the diameter `D` of the whole scatterer cloud (atoms and beads),
    bin the measured curve to the Shannon channels that diameter allows
    ([`Shannon.shannonData`](@ref BAYSOL.Utils.Shannon.shannonData))
    and pick the band limit from it
4.  build the forward-model (gram matrix) on the binned q grid
5.  produce a [`Inference.Seed`](@ref).

Arguments

  • molSrc::MolecularStructure.StructureSource: where to obtain the structure from (MolecularStructure.LocalPathSource, MolecularStructure.PDBIDSource, or MolecularStructure.URLSource).
  • energy::Real: beam energy in eV.
  • qvals::AbstractVector: momentum-transfer grid, Å⁻¹.
  • I_exp::AbstractVector, σ_exp::AbstractVector: measured intensity curve and its per-point standard errors; must be the same length as qvals. Pass the curve as measured: the part to fit is chosen here (qMin, qMax, excludeQ), it is binned here, and the bins with non-positive intensity are dropped here.
  • pH::Real: solution pH, drives the titration of Protein, DNA and RNA solutes and Pdb2pqr's hydrogen placement (when addHydrogens=true).
  • σ_pH::Real: standard uncertainty on pH, propagated through solute titration.
  • solutes::Vector{Solute}: the buffer's components, excluding the measured macromolecule (see Solute).

Keywords

  • rebin::Union{Nothing,Integer} = SHANNON_REBIN: bins per Shannon channel π/D (the bin width is π/(rebin·D), D the diameter of the scatterer cloud), at least this many: a precise curve gets finer bins, until the binning's worst-case bias is below maxBinBias of its smallest error bar (the number used is the report's rebin line); nothing fits the unbinned curve.

  • maxBinBias::Real = Shannon.BIN_BIAS_MAX: that bound, 0.3 by default; Inf keeps rebin as given. The data the fit used, the raw data and the choices made are in seed.shannon (Shannon.ShannonInfo).

  • lMax::Union{Nothing,Integer} = LMAX: spherical-harmonic band limit for the forward model (the setting LMAX); nothing, the default, takes ceil(qMax·D) (Shannon.autoLmax), qMax the largest binned q; an integer pins it.

  • dropNonpositive::Bool = true: drop the (binned) points whose intensity is not positive (Shannon.shannonData).

  • addHydrogens::Bool = ADD_HYDROGENS: whether to run PROPKA + Pdb2pqr at all (the setting ADD_HYDROGENS, true by default). false skips both and the forward model runs on the structure exactly as given (heavy-atom-only, unless the file already carries hydrogens). Modified nucleotides are then hydrogenated through their base, an approximation (Hydrogenator).

  • blmChunk::Unsigned = B_LM_CHUNK: Scattering.computeBLm batch size, forwarded to Scattering.forwardCache (results invariant, cost/memory tradeoff only).

  • thickness::Real = SHELL_THICKNESS, probe::Float64 = PROBE_RADIUS, nTarget::Union{Nothing,Int} = nothing: hydration-shell geometry, forwarded to Scattering.forwardCache.

  • t::Real = DEFAULT_TEMPERATURE_C: sample temperature in °C, forwarded to Inference.seedSampler.

  • κ_δρ₁₂::Real = κ_δρ₁₂, κ_δρ₃::Real = κ_δρ₃: concentrations of the δρ₁/δρ₂ and δρ₃ priors, forwarded to Inference.seedSampler.

  • pmvFallbackUncert::Real = PMV_FALLBACK_UNCERT: uncertainty, cm³ mol⁻¹, of a non-biological solute whose row of the partial-molar-volume table states none (default: the harvested mean of the table's stated ones, NONBIO_MEAN_UNCERTAINTY). Not recommended to change; it is recorded in the run log. Forwarded to Inference.seedSampler.

  • bounds_δρ₁₂ = BOUNDS_δρ₁₂, mode_δρ₁₂ = MODE_δρ₁₂: the support (low, high) of the δρ₁/δρ₂ priors (-10, 2) and their mode (1), in units of 0.03 e⁻/Å³; the mode must lie strictly inside the bounds.

  • φ_max = φ_max: the upper bound of the cavity occupancy (1.25), which sets the support of the δρ₃ prior.

  • exclVolCorrBounds = EXCL_VOL_CORR_BOUNDS, exclVolCorrEps = EXCL_VOL_CORR_EPS: the hard bounds of the profiled excluded-volume correction c1 ((0.8, 1.3)) and the padding and grid step of its search (0.02, at most the width of the bounds).

  • shellAreaPerPoint = SHELL_AREA_PER_POINT, shellMinPoints = SHELL_MIN_POINTS: Å² of accessible surface each shell bead stands for (4) and the least number of beads (55), forwarded to sasa (nTarget pins the number instead).

  • shellSample = SHELL_SAMPLE, sasaNOcc = SASA_N_OCC: directions sampled per atom (256) and the witness-pass prefix (104, at most shellSample), forwarded to sasa.

  • beadRayRange = BEAD_RAY_RANGE, beadRayDirs = BEAD_RAY_DIRS: range in Å (12) and number of directions (64) of the cavity classification of the beads, forwarded to sasa.

  • qMin = Q_MIN, qMax = Q_MAX: the q range fitted, Å⁻¹, both ends included; a point outside it is left out. nothing (the default) is no limit. qMin must be below qMax. The curve the range leaves is the measured curve of the run (seed.shannon): its binning, channels and band limit follow it.

  • sigmaScale = SIGMA_SCALE: the factor (above 0, 1 by default) the measured σ_exp are multiplied by, before the binning.

  • excludeQ = EXCLUDE_Q: ranges ((q_lo, q_hi), ...) of q, Å⁻¹, whose points are left out (both ends included); () by default. Each needs 0 ≤ q_lo < q_hi.

  • chains = CHAINS: the chain ids to keep, ("A", "B"); () (the default) keeps all. The others are removed before the hydrogens are added (MolecularStructure.keepChains).

  • model::Integer = MODEL: which model of a multi-model structure to use, counted from 1 in file order (1 is the first model whatever its number, the default and what was always read; MolecularStructure.resloveStruct).

The crop, the exclusion and the scale are applied to the measured curve first, before the structure is touched, so a range that leaves fewer than 3 points fails at once; the number of points that remain after the binning must be at least 3 too (the scale and the background are fitted). Waters are not part of the model (the hydration shell stands for them) and ligands, ions included, are, as heavy atoms without hydrogens (the loader keeps every non-water HETATM group, atoms of a known element), and which alternate location of an atom is used is that of BioStructures (the one of highest occupancy): neither is a keyword.

Every setting of loadSettings that the structure, shell and priors depend on is a keyword here: seedKeywords turns the settings into them. The keywords added after pmvFallbackUncert are checked as loadSettings checks the setting of the same name (the lower-case keyword is the setting's name, MAP_N_STARTS → mapNStarts), and a bad value throws the same InvalidSettingsError lines. With every keyword at its default the seed is what it was before they existed. A run that changes one of the data or structure keywords records it (qMin, qMax, sigmaScale, excludeQ, the number of points before and after, structureModel, structureChains, addHydrogens, lmaxPinned) in its log and workbook.

Returns

  • seed::Inference.Seed, ready to pass to runModel/Inference.infer.

Exceptions

  • MolecularStructure.ComponentError: a residue name is neither in the bundled component table nor in the cache and cannot be downloaded (offline).
  • DomainError: qvals, iExp, and σ_exp have mismatched lengths, or pmvFallbackUncert is not finite and above 0.
  • InvalidSettingsError: bounds_δρ₁₂, mode_δρ₁₂, φ_max, exclVolCorrBounds, exclVolCorrEps, shellAreaPerPoint, shellMinPoints, shellSample, sasaNOcc, beadRayRange or beadRayDirs, qMin, qMax, sigmaScale, excludeQ, chains or model is outside its limits or breaks a rule between them (mode_δρ₁₂ not strictly inside bounds_δρ₁₂, exclVolCorrEps above the width of exclVolCorrBounds, sasaNOcc above shellSample, qMin not below qMax, a range of excludeQ with q_lo not below q_hi), with the line loadSettings gives.
  • MolecularStructure.StructureSourceError: model is above the number of models of the structure, or chains names a chain it does not have (the message lists them).
  • ArgumentError: an input is outside its limits (a non-finite or non-positive q, σexp, energy, thickness or κ; a negative `σpHorprobe;rebin,lMax,nTargetorblmChunkbelow 1; a temperature at or below absolute zero), orqvals/Iexp/σexpare empty, or no point has a positive intensity after binning, or fewer than 3 points are left to fit byqMin,qMaxandexcludeQ` (before the binning) or by the binning and the dropping of non-positive bins (the message names those keywords).

BAYSOL.Pipeline._chisqMeasured — Method

The reduced χ² of the model curve y (on the fitted grid of sh) against the measured curve: y is interpolated onto the measured q points (Shannon.modelOnRaw) and the sum of squared normalized residuals is divided by the number of measured points less the three parameters fitted to the curve by minimization (scale, background and the excluded-volume correction c1). This is the χ² on the grid a program such as CRYSOL or FoXS evaluates, whatever binning the fit used.

The posterior band of the predicted curve at every q: for the draws curves[q, :] of each q, the pLo and pHi empirical quantiles and the smallest and largest draw inside them. BAYSOL.Pipeline.runModel — Function

Run NUTS on Inference._logπ starting from seed, returning posterior draws of the physical parameters ξ = (ρₑ, δρ₁, δρ₂, δρ₃), one (scale, bkgrndCorr) pair and predicted curve per draw, and AdvancedHMC.jl's diagnostics.

The Hamiltonian

HMC adds an auxiliary momentum r ~ N(0, M) (M the mass matrix) and defines a Hamiltonian:

H(θ, r) = -log π(θ) + ½ rᵀM⁻¹r

with potential energy as the negative log-posterior, and Gaussian kinetic energy. Leapfrog integration simulates the system's dynamics forward in time, alternating half-steps in r with full steps in θ:

r ← r - (ε/2)·∇[-log π(θ)]
θ ← θ + ε·M⁻¹r
r ← r - (ε/2)·∇[-log π(θ)]

which needs ∇[log π(θ)] at every step.

Because energy is conserved, leapfrog proposes long, correlated jumps through parameter space far more cheaply than random-walk Metropolis. NUTS removes the need to hand-pick a trajectory length: it grows the leapfrog trajectory by doubling a binary tree of steps, forward and backward in time, until the trajectory starts to double back on itself (a U-turn), then samples from the valid part of that tree.

MAP search and whitening

Before NUTS, Inference.infer finds the mode of the same log π with a multi-start L-BFGS search and takes a central-difference Hessian there. NUTS then samples coordinates w with θ = μ + σ·(ẑ + S·w), in which the posterior is ≈ N(0, I) and the chain starts at the mode. The map is affine, so the target distribution is unchanged; it only puts the adaptation below on the O(1) scales Stan's defaults assume. c1 is profiled out at every evaluation, to EXCL_VOL_CORR_TOL.

Step-size adaptation

δ is the target Metropolis acceptance rate. During the first nAdapt iterations, StepSizeAdaptor tunes the leapfrog step size ε via dual-averaging so the empirical acceptance rate converges to δ; too-small ε wastes computation taking tiny steps, too-large ε causes leapfrog's discretization error (and therefore the rejection rate) to blow up. A gradient that is inconsistent with the value (a loosely profiled c1, see EXCL_VOL_CORR_TOL) has the same effect and is the usual cause of a step size far below what the local curvature allows. MassMatrixAdaptor learns M (here the full parameter covariance, since ρₑ/δρ are physically coupled through the forward model) from the trajectory's sample covariance, starting from the identity in the whitened coordinates.

Arguments

  • seed::Seed: priors, initial point, forward cache, and data.
  • nSamples::Int64=N_ADAPT + N_DRAWS: total number of NUTS iterations (including the nAdapt warm-up steps); the default is 1000.
  • nAdapt::Int64=N_ADAPT: number of warm-up iterations spent adapting the step size and mass matrix before sampling proper; the default is 300, leaving N_DRAWS = 700 posterior draws.

Keywords

  • sigma::Union{Nothing,Real}=nothing: the half-width, in σ, of the interval of QuantileResult's "quantiles"/"bounds" entries: the empirical quantiles of the draws at the percentiles a Normal has at ±sigma σ (2 gives 2.28 – 97.72, 1 gives 15.87 – 84.13, 0.5 gives 30.85 – 69.15), any finite sigma > 0 (there is no upper limit: beyond about 3σ the quantiles of a few thousand draws rest on a handful of draws). The interval is that of the draws, not a Normal fitted to them, and the percentiles used are in the run sheet. nothing means VAL_SIGMA (2).

  • δ::Real=TARGET_ACCEPT: target acceptance rate as a percentage, (0, 100) exclusive (validated below); the default, TARGET_ACCEPT, is 95%.

  • rngSeed::Union{Nothing,Integer}=Inference.RNG_SEED (0): the run's random seed (see Inference.infer). To reproduce a fit exactly, pass the rngSeed row of its run log (or the run sheet); nothing picks a fresh one (so Random.seed! before the call also fixes the run). The default, 0, makes every run reproducible.

  • nChains::Int=Inference.N_CHAINS: number of NUTS chains (8; chain 1 at the MAP, the others at deterministic distances from it, in mirrored pairs); see Inference.infer. The draws of the chains that did not fail are pooled, and the report gives the split R̂ and ESS of the pool.

  • retry::Bool=Inference.RETRY (true; a CLI and developer switch, not a setting): rerun the sampler once, longer, always adapting again and from derived seeds, when the run does not count as converged (R̂ above Inference.RHAT_OK, an ESS below Inference.ESS_MIN, or either not computable); see Inference.infer. If the run is still not converged, runModel throws Inference.NotConvergedError and nothing is reported. retry = false skips both the rerun and the error: the run is only flagged in its summary.

  • retryNAdapt::Integer=Inference.RETRY_N_ADAPT, retryNDraws::Integer= Inference.RETRY_N_DRAWS (2000, 2000): warm-up and post-warm-up iterations per chain of the rerun.

  • chainStartRadii=Inference.CHAIN_START_RADII ((4, 10, 20)): the distances from the MAP, in posterior standard deviations, at which the mirrored pairs of chains start; pairs beyond the list reuse the last radius.

  • optimumChainCap::Integer=Inference.OPTIMUM_CHAIN_CAP (6), optimumMinChains::Integer=Inference.OPTIMUM_MIN_CHAINS (2), optimumChainRadius::Real=Inference.OPTIMUM_CHAIN_RADIUS (4), optimumChainFraction::Real=Inference.OPTIMUM_CHAIN_FRACTION (0.5): how many chains an optimum that carries mass should have, how many extra chains are started in all, and what counts as a chain being at an optimum (the share of its draws within the Mahalanobis radius).

  • mapMaxIter::Integer=Inference.MAP_MAX_ITER (500), mapNStarts::Integer= Inference.MAP_N_STARTS (64), mapFSuccessive::Integer=Inference.MAP_F_SUCCESSIVE (3), mapOptimaKept::Integer=Inference.MAP_OPTIMA_KEPT (8): the MAP search's iteration cap and number of L-BFGS starts, the successive small decreases that end a start, and the optima kept for comparison by Laplace mass.

  • mapJitterRadii=Inference.MAP_JITTER_RADII ((3, 6, 12)), mapJitterDirections::Integer=Inference.MAP_JITTER_DIRECTIONS (2), mapJitterMaxOptima::Integer=Inference.MAP_JITTER_MAX_OPTIMA (4), mapJitterWithinNats::Real=Inference.MAP_JITTER_WITHIN_NATS (15): the jittered starts around the optima found.

  • basinRestartNats::Real=Inference.BASIN_RESTART_NATS (2): how far above the MAP a draw must be for the sampler to re-whiten at its basin.

  • harvestCover::Integer=Inference.HARVEST_COVER (16), harvestDivergent::Integer=Inference.HARVEST_DIVERGENT (8): the draws, and divergent draws, from which L-BFGS looks for optima the search missed.

Every setting of loadSettings that the sampler depends on is a keyword here (nSamples and nAdapt are the positional arguments): runKeywords and runArguments turn the settings into them. The keywords from retryNAdapt on are those of Inference.SamplerOptions; they are checked as loadSettings checks the setting of the same name (the keyword is the setting's name in lower case, MAP_N_STARTS → mapNStarts) and a bad value throws the same InvalidSettingsError lines.

Returns

A 4-tuple (fit, divergencerate, map, curve):

  • fit::Inference.Inferred: the warm-up free posterior, the pooled chains' draws together.
  • divergenceRate::Float64: fraction of fit's draws AdvancedHMC.jl flagged as numerically divergent, [0, 1].
  • map/curve: either both nothing or a MAPResult/QuantileResult pair.

Exceptions

  • Inference.NotConvergedError if the run is not converged after its retry (retry = true): nothing is returned, so nothing can be reported.
  • DomainError if nAdapt < 1, nSamples ≤ nAdapt, sigma is not a finite number above 0, or δ is not strictly inside (0, 100); nChains < 1.
  • InvalidSettingsError if one of the keywords from retryNAdapt on is outside its limits (a count below its least, a radius at or below 0, a fraction outside (0, 1], a chainStartRadii or mapJitterRadii that is not three positive numbers).
